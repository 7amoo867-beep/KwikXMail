A Temporary Email Address

Use a temporary email address for suitable situations, such as low-risk sign-ups, instead of sharing your primary address every time. Temporary email is not a suitable replacement for important accounts or sensitive communications.

Check for Incoming Messages

The page can periodically check for new messages while your inbox is active. Delivery time depends on the sender and the email service being used. Automatic OTP extraction is not currently available.

Available Email Domains

The email addresses currently available depend on the third-party email service used by this website. Available domains may change, and some websites may reject temporary email addresses.

Keep Your Main Inbox Organized

Temporary email can help separate some low-priority sign-ups from your primary inbox and reduce the marketing messages sent there. Use it only where the other service permits temporary addresses.

Use Temporary Email Responsibly

Temporary email can be useful for simple, permitted use cases and testing, but it does not guarantee complete anonymity or override another website's rules. Some services may block temporary addresses.

Understand the Privacy Limits

This website currently relies on a third-party email API, so you should not assume messages are never stored or permanently erased when you close the page. Do not use temporary email for passwords, financial information, or other sensitive data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a temporary email?

It is an email address used to receive messages temporarily. It can help you avoid sharing your primary address for some low-priority sign-ups.

Is temporary email safe to use?

It may be suitable for some simple use cases, but not for sensitive information or important accounts. Do not assume messages are completely private or immediately deleted from the service provider's servers.

Why would I use a temporary email?

It can be useful for low-risk sign-ups and permitted testing, helping you keep them separate from your primary inbox. Some websites do not accept temporary email addresses.

Can I send messages from this email?

No. The current website functionality is designed for receiving and viewing messages only.

Does the site automatically extract verification codes?

That feature is not currently available. You can open a message and read its content manually if it arrives in your inbox.

Does clearing an address permanently delete messages from the server?

In the current version, the Clear button removes the email details from the page and stops inbox updates. This alone does not guarantee that the account or messages are deleted from the email provider's servers.
